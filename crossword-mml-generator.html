Crossword builder.

Create a grid, choose Across or Down, click a starting square, and type a word. Press Enter to finish it. Click another square to start the next word.

01 / Grid

Creating a new grid clears the current puzzle.

02 / Type into the grid

Click a square and type. Backspace erases a letter; Enter ends the word. Click an existing word to edit it. Click its starting square again to switch direction. Black squares appear when you finish.

03 / Clues

Clue fields appear automatically as you add words. Starting squares receive standard crossword numbers in grid order.
